# Test TICs, off-centre axes, family-only at play 0.03 / bevel 0.04

**Date:** 2026-11-03 · **Code:** branch `rotation-moves/7-model-in-file` at
2eaddbb7 (plus uncommitted model-in-file work, geometry unchanged), release
build (`just build-release`). **Puzzles:** `puzzles/BTFiles/test_tics/` (not
in the repository): TIC 28 Eaten Strawberry Shortcake, TIC 2.99 Clutch 4,
TIC 2.9 Clutch 3, and `L 36`, `L 41`, `L 42` (the L files leave the result
shape all variable; L 41 has no snapshots). All except L 41 are solution
files with one snapshot per step, so `xcheck` / `diag` work on them.

**Question:** they are supposed to come apart without extra play and bevel.
Do we need turns about axes that are neither a cube centre nor a cube edge?
Target (the user): the fixed path family only, no planner, at play 0.03 /
bevel 0.04.

The scenes (diag `DUMP` format) and the tools used (`famtest.cpp`,
`need.cpp`, `diag.cpp` `MCHECK` and `T<k>`, `xcheck.cpp`
`MODEL`/`START`/`EXTEND`) are not in the repository: they are on the local
branch `rotation-study-data`, under `design/rotation-study/`. Names below
refer to them.

## Summary

1. **A fixed off-centre axis is not what the TICs lack.** At the printed
   model (0.0126 / 0.032) the blocked turn of TIC 28, Clutch 4 and Clutch 3
   is cleared by a planner path polished on a finer grid (`need REFINE`):
   the planner's grid resolution, not the motion class, was the limit.
2. **At 0.03 / 0.04 those three come apart with the current wide family**,
   no planner needed.
3. **The L puzzles do not**, for two reasons: (a) 9 turns on their route
   need paths outside the family: 6 are covered by larger wiggles or more
   ramp holds, 3 (one turn type) only by a shift followed by a turn about an
   off-centre point (here the off-centre idea does help), or a two-leg path;
   (b) L 36's last separation needs more than 0.03 / 0.04 by any motion found.
   L 42's last pair and L 41 are not understood yet.

## 1. Off-centre axes on random scenes (printed model 0.0126 / 0.032)

240 random planar scenes (moving polyomino of 1–4 cells, random static cells
within 3.6 of a lattice pivot, end pose free). Certified smallest clearance
of: the pure turn, the best of the wide family (881 paths), the planner, and
turns about the lattice pivot + e (then shifted straight back onto the
lattice, or shifted first so the turn lands on it), banded by max(|e_u|,
|e_v|). Off-centre turns that pass through a whole-cell offset (a lattice
turn plus a slide) were excluded. The tool (`offaxis.cpp`) was lost with a
session scratch directory; `famtest OFF=1` covers the same motions on given
scenes.

| motion | scenes clear | clears what the family can't | clears what the planner can't |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure lattice turn | 18 | – | – |
| wide family | 45 | – | – |
| planner (shifts ≤ 0.5) | 50 | – | – |
| off-centre, e ≤ 0.1 | 19 | 0 | 0 |
| off-centre, e ≤ 0.25 | 22 | 1 | 0 |
| off-centre, e ≤ 0.5 | 46 | 5 | 4 |
| off-centre, e ≤ 0.75 | 48 | 7 | 6 |

Slightly off-centre turns never beat the planner (they lie inside its shift
box). The far ones that do need shifts of 0.85–1.5 cells, with the axis near
a neighbouring lattice pivot: a lattice turn plus a near-whole-cell move
between grid positions, outside the planner's box; only in sparse scenes.

## 2. The TICs' blocked turns at the printed model (0.0126 / 0.032)

`FROM=1 PARTIAL=1 MODEL=0.0126,0.032 xcheck` finds the step none of our
searches makes; scenes `TIC28_12`, `Clutch4_21`, `Clutch3_23`. Clearances:

| turn | pure | best off-centre | family | planner | planner 0.005/360 | planner 0.0025/720 | polished |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TIC 28, 11→12 | −0.170 | −0.106 | −0.006 | −0.005 | −0.0020 | −0.0011 | **+0.0003** |
| Clutch 4, 20→21 | −0.170 | −0.106 | −0.056 | −0.004 | −0.0023 | −0.0007 | **+0.00015** |
| Clutch 3, 22→23 | −0.170 | −0.106 | −0.006 | −0.006 | −0.0025 | −0.0014 | **+0.0005** |

The planner's optimistic bound shrank with the grid (+0.015 → +0.008 →
+0.0045). Its paths are diagonal wiggles: a turn about an axis that moves
during the turn. Polished = planner path resampled to 16–24 waypoints,
hill-climbed on the sampled clearance, certified with `minClearance`. So the
planner cannot resolve gaps of about 0.001; a local polish after the grid
search would fix that.

## 3. Family only at 0.03 / 0.04

`burrTxt -d -R -m 0.03,0.04`: TIC 28 level 18, 3 turns; Clutch 4 30.8, 14
turns; Clutch 3 1.1.1.23.4, 8 turns (about 1.6 s each). L 36, L 41, L 42: no
disassembly.

### Turns on the L route (`diag MCHECK=0.03,0.04` per step)

Blocked for the family (all cleared by the planner at about +0.025):

| scene | turn | best family path (clearance) | planner |
|---|---|---|---|
| L36_14, L42_7, L42_11 | Z −90 about centre (3, −1) | wiggle 0.55 (−0.004 / −0.010) | +0.028 |
| L36_24 | X +90 about centre (−2, 5) | wiggle 0.55 (−0.004) | +0.029 |
| L42_10 | Z −90 about corner (3.5, −1.5) | ramp 0.55 (−0.0008) | +0.030 |
| L42_17, L42_20 | Z +90 about centre (2, −1) | wiggle 0.55 (−0.004) | +0.026 |
| L42_22 | Y −90 about corner (1.5, 1.5) | ramp 0.25 (−0.007) | +0.030 |
| L42_33 | Y −90 about centre (4, −1) | wiggle 0.55 (−0.004) | +0.024 |

What clears them (`famtest 0.03 0.04 scenes-2026-11-03/L*_[0-9]*.txt`, smallest amount):

* wiggle 0.60–0.65: L36_24, L42_10, L42_17, L42_20, L42_33;
* ramp out by 15°, hold to 75°, amount 0.40: L36_24, L42_10, L42_17, L42_20;
* ramp 60–75 at 0.30, 75–75 at 0.35: L42_22;
* nothing of one direction (amounts to 1.0, any 15° holds): **L36_14, L42_7,
  L42_11**. These clear with `TWO=1` (e.g. out to direction 10 at 0.3 by
  15°, to direction 13 at 0.3 by 60°, back by 90°) and with `OFF=1`: shift
  s ≈ 0.5–0.6 cells, then turn about pivot + e with e ≈ 0.3 along s (about
  halfway), then shift back. A pure off-centre turn (s = 0) clears none of
  them; the s = 0 hits elsewhere are e = (±0.5, ±0.5), the neighbouring
  corner pivot.

`EXTEND=1 xcheck` (wide family + wiggles 0.6–0.7, ramps 15–75 0.3–0.4,
60–75/15–30 and 75–75/15–15 at 0.3–0.35, 1105 paths): L 36 and L 42 still do
not come apart, from any snapshot, because of the last pair.

### The last pair

* **L 36** (snapshot 37, pieces a and c): `diag T37` finds three turns of c
  about X that only a planner could make (`L36_T37_*`). The best,
  `L36_T37_1` (corner pivot (3.5, 3.5)): planner −0.0089 at 0.03 / 0.04,
  polished −0.005; `requiredPlay` at bevel 0.04: 0.0103–0.0378; at play
  0.03 the planner clears it from bevel 0.06 (+0.003). No fixed shape
  (`famtest`, all three modes) clears it even at bevel 0.06. At play 0.1 /
  bevel 0.1 the pair separates with one turn and a slide; at play 0.1 /
  bevel 0.04, with or without the planner, it does not (the search plans
  only turns the family clears with `slack` 0.02 more play).
* **L 42** (snapshot 43): no single turn that only the planner makes; the
  pair does not come apart at any play up to 0.1 at bevel 0.04. Not
  investigated further.
* **L 41**: no snapshots; not investigated.

## Next steps

* Decide whether to extend the family: wiggles to 0.65, ramps 15–75, and a
  new kind "shift, then turn about an off-centre point" (or two-leg paths).
  The family must stay closed under reversal (add each path's reverse), and
  the table build is the main cost.
* L 36's last turn: at 0.03 / 0.04 it seems to need slightly more (play
  ≈ 0.038 or bevel 0.06, proven); a finer polish over the full 0.5 box was
  still −0.005.
* L 42's last pair (multi-move?), L 41 (run the assembler, then `diag T<k>`
  needs a snapshot: write the assembly as one).
* Planner: polish its path locally after the grid search (section 2).

## Reproducing

```bash
# from a checkout of rotation-study-data; build each tool as in ../README.md, e.g.
c++ -I build-rel -I src -I subprojects/manifold-3.5.2/include -DNDEBUG -std=c++20 -O2 -pthread \
    design/rotation-study/scripts/famtest.cpp -o famtest \
    -Wl,--start-group build-rel/libburr_core.a build-rel/subprojects/manifold-3.5.2/libmanifold.a -lz -Wl,--end-group
cd puzzles/BTFiles/test_tics
MODEL=0.03,0.04 FROM=1 PARTIAL=1 xcheck "TIC 28 - Eaten Strawberry Shortcake.xmpuzzle"   # blocked step
MCHECK=0.03,0.04 diag "L 42.R15.44.R15-....xmpuzzle" 7                                     # family vs planner
DUMP=1 diag "<file>" <k>                                                                    # scene file
DUMPDIR=dir TAG=L36 diag "L 36....xmpuzzle" T37                                             # turns at a snapshot
famtest 0.03 0.04 scenes/*.txt; TWO=1 famtest ...; OFF=1 LIST=1 famtest ...
need scene.txt 0.04 0.03; REFINE=16 need scene.txt 0.032 0.0126
EXTEND=1 START=37 MODEL=0.03,0.04 xcheck "L 36....xmpuzzle"
burrTxt -d -R -m 0.03,0.04 "<file>"
```
